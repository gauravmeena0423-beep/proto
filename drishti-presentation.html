01 · Problem Statement
The Challenges
Karnataka Police Faces Today
📝
Manual FIR Filing is Slow & Error-Prone
Officers spend hours manually drafting FIRs without guidance on correct BNS penal sections — leading to legal errors and case weaknesses.
🗂️
No Cross-Case Criminal Intelligence
Repeat offenders and criminal syndicates go undetected because FIRs are siloed across stations with no automated linkage or network analysis.
🗺️
Reactive Patrolling — Not Predictive
Beat patrols are assigned based on intuition, not data-driven hotspot intelligence. High-risk zones go under-patrolled during peak crime hours.
🌐
Language Barrier in Multi-Lingual Jurisdiction
Officers and complainants speak Kannada and Hindi, but most intelligence tools are English-only — creating friction and errors in reporting.
⚖️
Weak Evidentiary Documentation for Courts
Case files lack proper Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023 classification — making digital evidence inadmissible or challenged in court.
🔒
No Statutory Audit Trail
Access to sensitive victim data, classified FIRs and intelligence queries goes unlogged — violating DPDPA 2023 and exposing departments to liability.

09 · Legal Compliance Framework
100% Statutory Compliant
with Indian Law 2023
✅
Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023
AI penal code suggestions mapped exclusively to BNS sections (replaces IPC). Charges auto-applied to e-FIR.
✅
Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023
e-FIR workflow strictly follows BNSS §173 statutory registration procedure with sealed receipt.
✅
Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023
Court dossier classifies all evidence by admissibility: Fact vs. Inference. Digital chain-of-custody maintained.
✅
Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023
All PII access is role-gated. Survivor identity suppression toggle. Every data access event SHA-256 logged.
✅
Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO)
Minor victim/survivor identities are automatically flagged for suppression regardless of officer role.
⚖️ Explainable AI (XAI) Principle
Every AI output is explicitly classified:
FACT — Verified SCRB source record
INFERENCE — Algorithmic analytical link
PREDICTION — Probabilistic forecast

🏆 Key Technical Decisions
No Framework — Pure Vanilla JS for maximum stability, zero dependency risk, and complete offline capability without npm/build tools.

CDN-Pinned Versions — All library versions are pinned (not @latest) to prevent unexpected breaking changes in a police-critical system.

Single Codebase — 9 HTML pages share 2 JS files and 5 CSS files. No duplication, fully maintainable by a small team.

Gemini API Direct — No backend proxy. API key stays in officer's browser localStorage only.
✅ Zero Backend ✅ Offline-First ✅ No npm/Node.js ✅ Air-Gap Safe
